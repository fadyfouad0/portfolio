Cairo, EG · UI/UX Design
Hospitality · Fractional ownership

Green City.

The brief

Green City is the first and leading fractional-ownership resort brand in Egypt, four resorts, one week of stay every year.

Overview

In 2024 my mentor handed me Green City as a case study, a full redesign of the website they already had live. The brand was selling fractional ownership across four resorts in Egypt, but the existing site buried that offer. I owned the work end to end: research and ideation, then a design system to build on, then a redesign of the whole site, made responsive from desktop down to mobile.

// 01 · Problem

A premium offer, buried by a cluttered site.

The old website fought its own brand. A weak visual hierarchy, overwhelming image clutter and poor content organisation made a genuinely premium offer feel untrustworthy, and added friction to every step of the booking journey.

The job was to make four resorts feel credible and easy to explore in a single scroll, and to earn enough trust that a first-time visitor would convert.

// 02 · User persona

Two people had to trust it before booking.

Persona 01

Adel

Retired · Family man · Cairo

Values peace, comfort and legacy. Wants a trustworthy, simple way to explore resorts and enjoy his yearly stay, an investment he can confidently pass to his children.

Persona 02

Rana

Sales manager & mother · 32

Plans the family’s holidays around a busy schedule. Needs to compare resorts fast, book easily from mobile, and feel reassured by clear trust signals.

Each screen sells the stay the way the resorts do, calm, visual, one decision at a time.

Lead with the place, then the promise.

An immersive, trustworthy hero

A full-bleed hero sets the mood and drops you straight into a vacation before a word is read. One clear line, "the first and leading company in fractional ownership", establishes credibility instantly, with "Book Now" and "Learn More" set side by side so the next move is never in doubt.

Resorts, one tap away

A tabbed "Our Resorts" block lets visitors switch between Al Hayah, El Nour Plaza, Sunny Lakes and Red Sea View without leaving the page, paired with customer reviews that reassure before they commit.
