// Overview

FILE · 01 / LUMENS

A creative agency site that puts the work centre-stage and keeps the next step one click away.

Lumens Studios works across the MENA region: branding, web design, content production and digital marketing under one roof. The brief was a marketing site that presents a wide range of services without overwhelming visitors or flattening the studio's personality. I led the information architecture, visual hierarchy, layout system and prototyping.

01 The problem

Creative agencies often struggle to clearly communicate their value.

Visitors land on visually appealing websites but leave without understanding what the agency actually does, how it's different from others, or whether they can trust it with their brand.

As a result, potential clients hesitate and delay taking action, the work looks good, but the path forward is unclear.

Beautiful work, blurry intent. Visitors leave admiring, not converting. Audit finding · pre-design
02 The approach

Make the studio's identity impossible to miss.

Every interaction, from landing on the homepage to exploring a case study, was designed to feel natural and seamless. Visual hierarchy, color and layout were structured to highlight the work and expertise while keeping the personality intact.

Services were grouped without diluting any one offering, and meaningful actions, contact the team, request a quote, were surfaced at every natural stopping point, not buried at the end.

Three guardrails shaped every screen: identity always on, services always discoverable, conversion always one click away.

04Homepage
Land, understand, act.

The Homepage is structured so a ten-second scroll explains the studio.

Below the hero, three coloured buttons, View services, Learn about us, View our work, split visitors by intent and send each one straight to the page that matches their question.

The persistent Reach Out button in the lime pill sits in the same place on every page, a single, friendly CTA instead of three competing ones.

05About

Identity, front-loaded.

The About page turns interest into trust, introducing the team, values and process behind the work so a curious visitor leaves feeling they know who they'd be working with.
lumens.studio / about
HOVER TO SCROLL

Structured to be read, not skimmed.

I built the About page as a single vertical narrative so the studio's story unfolds in a deliberate order: who they are, what they believe, then how they work. Each section is its own full-width band, so the eye always knows where one idea ends and the next begins.

To keep long-form copy readable, I set a generous type measure, broke dense paragraphs into single-idea blocks, and used the lime accent sparingly to mark the moments that matter, guiding attention without a hard sell and leaving the persistent Reach Out CTA one click away throughout.

06Services

Seven offers, one shelf.

The Services page is the studio's menu. Seven offers, from brand systems to digital campaigns, sit on a single page so a visitor can scan the range in one scroll. Each card numbers itself, leads with the product name, and follows the same anatomy: photo · title · category · five-line value pitch.
lumens.studio / services
HOVER TO SCROLL

A menu, not a brochure.

The hardest problem here was range: seven services could easily read as clutter. So I opened the page with one clear promise, creative direction with strategic insight, giving the eye a single confident thing to land on before any choice appears.

Then I made a deliberate call, present the services as a numbered list rather than a grid of tiles. One shared card template repeats down the page, so a visitor compares offers instead of re-learning a new layout each time. The numbering turns browsing into a sense of progress, and gives each service the dignity of its own line.

07Service pages · 01–07

One template, seven dedicated pages.

Every service in the Lumens catalogue earns its own page — same dark surface, same lime accent, same five-block anatomy. Pick one to load the page on the right; the rest follow the same template, swapping only the photography and the conditional CTA.
