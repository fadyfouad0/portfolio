InOfficePlus.

The brief

InOfficePlus is an Employer of Record and payroll service that lets global companies hire in Egypt & South Africa, without a local legal entity.

Overview

The owner of InOfficePlus reached out to me to design their first website and handed me the brief. From there the work was mine end to end. I ran the research and the user interviews, then built a design system so the whole thing could stay consistent as it grew. I designed the full site on top of it, home through pricing and contact, made it hold up from desktop down to mobile, and kept iterating with the client until every page said what it needed to.

// 01 · Problem

Navigating the complexity of global hiring.

Companies that hire talent in foreign markets face tangled legal, compliance, and administrative challenges. Managing employment contracts, local labor laws, and payroll without a local entity is time-consuming, risky, and prone to errors.

InOfficePlus solves that. My job was to design their website from scratch, making a genuinely complex service feel obvious in a single scroll, and earning enough trust that a stranger abroad would book a call.

// 02 · Who it's for

Two people had to see themselves in the first scroll.

Persona 01

Persona 02

// 04 · The build

Every page answers one question, then earns the next scroll.

inofficeplus.com
Hover to scroll
Home

A homepage built to convert.

One clear next step

Rather than crowd the hero with features, it leads with a single message and one prominent "Book a Call" button, so the primary action is obvious before a visitor scrolls a pixel.

The full story on one page

The homepage previews both services, then backs them with a partner logo strip and an FAQ section, so a visitor sees what's offered, who trusts it, and gets their objections answered, all before deciding to book a call.

inofficeplus.com/services
Hover to scroll
Services

A guided flow that turns complexity into simple steps.

Services, clearly separated

EOR and Payroll are laid out as two distinct, self-contained tracks with short explanations, so visitors quickly find the path that fits instead of wading through one dense block.

How it works, in three beats

The process is broken into a short, linear sequence. By showing what happens first, second, and third, the UI removes uncertainty and answers the unspoken question: "what happens after I contact you?"

The two services

Two doors into the same promise.

Payroll

Service 01

EOR

inofficeplus.com/eor
Hover to scroll

    Hire without a local entity

    The page leads with the core promise, InOfficePlus becomes the legal employer on the client's behalf, so they can build a team in a new market without setting one up.

inofficeplus.com/pricing
Hover to scroll
Pricing

Costs that adapt to the region.

Region-aware by default

Flag icons for Egypt and South Africa let visitors self-identify their market and update the compliance checklist, while a USD / EUR / ZAR / EGP switcher removes any mental conversion.

Plans built from your choices

Pick a region, then a service, and the relevant plans appear, so visitors only ever see pricing that applies to their exact situation instead of one long, generic table.

Optional add-ons

Extras can be layered on top of the base plan, letting clients tailor the service to what they actually need and see how each add-on changes the price.

Book a call

Turn intent into a booked call.

// One destination

Every Book a Call across the site, in the nav, the hero, the service and pricing pages, leads here, so no matter where intent strikes, the visitor lands on one clear place to schedule, and the booking itself stays obvious and simple, turning intent into a confirmed call with minimal friction.
